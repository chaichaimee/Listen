Listen


The ultimate seamless audio companion designed to let you preview, control, and navigate your audio playlist effortlessly from anywhere on your system.


author: chai chaimee

url: https://github.com/chaichaimee/Listen

Description

Listen transforms the way you interact with audio files on your computer. Instead of opening bulky, distracting media players every time you want to review an audio track, Listen embeds a powerful, lightweight background audio engine directly into your NVDA screen reader. Whether you are dealing with massive folders of sound effects, lectures, music, or recordings, this add-on gives you the freedom to control your audio on the fly without ever losing your place or interrupting your workflow.


Hot Keys

The primary gateway to managing your audio is the Master Control Key. This key behaves differently depending on how many times you press it in quick succession:

Master Control Key: Alt + Windows + L

  • Single Tap: Activates Listen Mode on a selected file, or instantly wakes up/brings back control of your background music from anywhere on your PC.
  • Double Tap: Permanently stops the current audio, saves your position, and completely closes the media layer.

Listen Mode Control Map (Active Only Inside Listen Mode)

Once Listen Mode is active, your keyboard temporarily transforms into an interactive media console. The following dedicated single-key commands become available:

  • Escape or Q: Hides the Listen Control Mode so you can resume normal computer tasks while the audio keeps playing smoothly in the background.
  • Spacebar or C: Toggle Play / Pause.
  • Right Arrow: Fast forward by 10 seconds.
  • Left Arrow: Rewind by 10 seconds.
  • Page Up: Increase volume.
  • Page Down: Decrease volume.
  • Up Arrow: Skip to the Previous audio file in the folder.
  • Down Arrow: Skip to the Next audio file in the folder.
  • X: Restart the current track from the very beginning.
  • Z: Clear your entire playback history and saved positions.
  • W: Announce the current playback position.
  • R: Announce the remaining time left on the track.
  • T: Announce the total duration of the track.
  • E: Instantly skip ahead to the last 10 seconds of the audio track.
  • B: Drop a custom smart bookmark at the current playback timestamp.
  • Ctrl + B: Jump directly forward to your next saved bookmark.
  • Shift + B: Jump directly backward to your previous saved bookmark.

Features

1. The Zero-Interruption Folder Workflow

Managing massive audio collections can be exhausting when you have to open and close files one by one. Listen completely fixes this hassle by grouping entire folders automatically.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Open File Explorer and browse to any folder containing audio tracks.
  2. Focus on any supported audio file (such as MP3, WAV, WMA, M4A, FLAC, AAC, OGG, OPUS, or MIDI).
  3. Press Alt + Windows + L (Single Tap). You will hear a sharp confirmation tone and NVDA will say "Listen Mode Active". Your audio starts playing immediately.
  4. Want to hear the next track? Simply press the Down Arrow. Want to go back? Press the Up Arrow. Listen will swap files instantly while keeping your focus comfortably right inside your folder.

2. Ambient Playback and Intelligent Window Hiding

Need to take notes or read a document while listening to an audio file? Listen lets you put the playback system into the background without interrupting the sound.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. While listening to a track inside Listen Mode, press Q or Escape. You will hear a lower tone and NVDA will announce "Listen Mode Hidden".
  2. Your audio continues playing seamlessly, but your keyboard is instantly released back to normal, allowing you to type, navigate webs, or fill out forms.
  3. Alternatively, simply pressing Alt + Tab to look at another application window will automatically hide Listen Mode, putting safety locks on your media hotkeys so you never press them by accident.

3. Global Remote Control From Anywhere

What if you are working deep inside a Word document or a web browser, and you want to pause, rewind, or skip to the next audio track without navigating back to your original folder?

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. No matter what program or window you are currently using, press Alt + Windows + L (Single Tap).
  2. Listen instantly wakes up and reconnects your keyboard directly to your running audio file. You will hear the high-pitched tone and NVDA will say "Listen Mode Active".
  3. You can now immediately use any of the media control keys (e.g., Spacebar to pause, Left/Right Arrows to skip through time, or Up/Down Arrows to swap tracks inside that remote folder).
  4. Once you are done adjusting, press Q or Escape to immediately lock your keys back to your document text. You never have to interrupt your visual focus or change your working window.

4. Smart Memory: Automatic Resume & History Management

Never lose your spot again. Listen acts like an intelligent bookkeeper that watches over your position and audio preferences.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. If you need to stop your audio entirely, press Alt + Windows + L twice (Double Tap). You will hear a low buzz and NVDA will report "Permanently Stopped".
  2. The add-on instantly remembers your exact second in that track, along with your volume settings.
  3. The next time you return to that file and trigger it with a single tap, it will pick up right where you left off.
  4. If your history gets too cluttered or you want to review tracks fresh from the start, press Z while in active Listen Mode to wipe your playback memory clean instantly.

5. Precise Timestamp Bookmarking

When reviewing long recordings or lectures, dropping anchors at vital spots is essential. Listen lets you save unlimited custom points within any track.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. While listening to an active file, press B at any exact moment you hear something important. A chime will play, letting you know your bookmark is saved.
  2. To quickly scan through your important highlights, press Ctrl + B to fly forward to the next bookmark, or Shift + B to travel backward to previous bookmarks.
